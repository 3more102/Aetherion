Aetherion is an educational project for building and training a causal language model from scratch. It uses attention, token embeddings, and next-token prediction. Engineers write tests before increasing model size. Researchers measure validation loss on held-out data. The sample data contains no private personal information. This sample demonstrates a training pipeline and cannot produce a capable general-purpose assistant.
